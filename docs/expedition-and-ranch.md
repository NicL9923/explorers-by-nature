# A picnic at Pinewatch

The arrival trail now has a picnic basket. Press E beside it to pack a shared picnic, follow the trail north to Pinewatch Overlook, and press E at the picnic blanket. After five quiet seconds, the shared journal records your discovery of alpine flower seeds. Return to the arrival basket and press E to unlock planting alpine flowers for everyone on the ranch.

The outing has no timer, food cost, spoilage or animal-neglect penalty. Escape cancels the picnic pause. Everyone shares progress, so one person can pack while another completes the picnic or brings the seeds home. Progress and the flower unlock survive server restarts and solo reloads. Existing saves start at the packing step; their buildings and pantry are preserved.

Building now includes a timber bench, post lantern, water trough, planted flower box and stone-ring campfire. B opens building, [ and ] browse the entire palette, 1–6 select the original building pieces, R rotates, M moves and right-click removes. Alpine flowers join the palette after the outing. Green previews can be placed; red previews explain why placement is refused. The preview and server share placement rules, while the server checks the final command again to handle simultaneous builders.

The props are original procedural geometry with reusable materials. Lanterns and campfires use emissive surfaces and a shared pool of at most four nearby shadowless point lights during evening. The pool chooses the nearest props every half second, keeping large family builds affordable. Fire is harmless. Press E at a bench to sit and look around; E, Space or a movement key stands you back at your approach position. Removing the occupied bench also returns you safely. Milking and egg gathering confirm the actual saved pantry increase and play feedback after authority accepts it.

Validation: the server suite exercises picnic ordering and location checks, duplicate claim rejection, persistence, and placement/removal of each new prop. Unity builds and rendered verification are recorded in the main validation ledger once completed.
